# How to use Waraq

## Install the app on your phone

Open your Waraq address over HTTPS in Chrome, Edge or Safari.

- **Android (Chrome or Edge):** tap the install banner, or menu > **Install app**.
- **iPhone and iPad (Safari):** Share > **Add to Home Screen**.

The installed app opens full screen, keeps working offline for pages you have visited, and shows a prompt when a new version is available.

## Scan a document

1. Tap **Scan** (the large button in the bottom bar, or **Scan document** in the sidebar on desktop).
2. Point the camera at the paper. Waraq outlines the page it detects. Tap the shutter, or turn on auto capture in Settings > Scanner to let it shoot when the outline is steady.
3. In batch mode keep capturing pages, then tap the thumbnail strip to review.
4. For each page you can drag the crop corners, rotate, and pick a filter: original, magic color, grayscale, black and white, or lighten.
5. Tap **Save**. Waraq uploads in the background and builds the PDF. You can keep scanning while it works.

If the camera is unavailable, Waraq offers to pick photos from the gallery instead.

## Import files

- **Import** on the library screen accepts PDFs and images (JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC where the browser can decode it).
- On Android, use **Share** in another app and choose Waraq to send PDFs or images straight into the library.
- Drag and drop files onto the library on desktop.

## Find documents

The search field matches titles and OCR text in Arabic and English. Narrow results with folders, tags and the starred filter, and sort by date modified, date created, title or size.

OCR runs in the background after a document is created. The badge on a document shows when it is text searchable. Settings > OCR chooses the languages and their order, and can re-run OCR for everything.

## Organize

- **Folders** can be nested. Move documents from the document menu or by multi-select in the library.
- **Tags** are free text. Tap a tag to filter.
- **Star** what you need often. Starred documents have their own tab.
- **Trash** keeps deleted documents for 30 days by default (7 to 90 days in Settings > Storage), then removes them for good. Restore puts a document back; delete permanently or empty the trash to remove them sooner.

## Edit pages

Open a document, then:

- tap **Select** to rotate, delete, move or extract pages
- drag pages to reorder them
- open a page and tap edit to re-crop it or change its filter
- **Add pages** appends new scans or files
- the document menu has split, merge, duplicate and compress

## Share a document

1. Open the document and tap **Share**.
2. Create a link. Choose how long it lasts (1 hour, 1 day, 7 days, 30 days or never), whether a password is needed, and whether viewers may download the PDF.
3. Copy the link or show its QR code.

Anyone with an active link can view the document without an account. Settings > Sharing lists every link with its view count. Revoke one link, all links of a document, or every link at once.

You can also send the PDF itself through the system share sheet without creating a link.

## Export and print

**Export** in the document view produces a PDF (optionally password protected), a zip of JPEG or PNG pages, or the OCR text. Pick all pages or a selection. **Print** opens a print-ready view.

## Back up to Google Drive (optional)

With Google configured (see [Google OAuth](GOOGLE-OAUTH.md)), open Settings > Google Drive and tap **Connect**. Waraq creates a `Waraq` folder in your Drive and keeps a PDF copy of every document there, mirroring your folders. Waraq only sees files it created. Trashed documents move to `Waraq/Trash`, and permanently deleted ones go to the Drive trash. Your server copy is always the original.

## Sign-in options

You always sign in with your username and password. You may also link one Google account in Settings > Account, then use **Continue with Google** on the sign-in page. Google never creates accounts in Waraq. Settings > Devices lists active sessions and signs out other devices.
